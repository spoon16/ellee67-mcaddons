# Notices

ElleeDog 67 Pets is an unofficial test add-on, not an official Minecraft product and
not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.

The native player behavior/client snapshots, Persona third-person controller
extracts and native cape renderer snapshot in `upstream/` originate from
Mojang/bedrock-samples. Those native portions are retained in the generated packs
with the documented changes. See `upstream/LICENSE.md` and the provenance records
for the pinned commit, retrieval/extraction notes and original notices.

Carter's Cavalier King Charles Spaniel geometry, coat texture and base icon are the
ElleeDog 67 assets previously authored in this conversation. This revision does not
change the dog's geometry or coat. Diagnostic marker textures and version captions
are generated by the included build tool. The native cape texture/geometry aliases
are referenced; no player's actual cape or Marketplace assets are copied into this
pack.

Historical cav: item/entity/save aliases are retained only for compatibility. All
current commands use pet:. No font files, user screenshots, custom skin packs or
Marketplace models/textures are distributed. No in-game validation is claimed.

0.2.1 additionally retains semantic native hand-bone and first-person swap-animation
excerpts as documented in upstream/grip_fix.PROVENANCE.json. The empty-handed Carter
branch of the namespaced swap animation is an adaptation of the referenced native
animation. The model's visible dog cubes and coat are retained, while its invisible
compatibility skeleton has changed. The source package contains no font files.

## 0.3.0 additions

Mochi geometry, its tuxedo coat, paws, and the pet-specific armor-fit shapes were
newly authored for this project. Carter's visible cube geometry and pixels remain
unchanged except for normalization of internal bone names for the shared rig.
Native hand/animation conventions and armor adapter wiring are adapted from the
Mojang references listed in SOURCES.md. The source-only iron texture used by offline
previews is covered by the upstream notices, not by a new license grant. Neither
source nor installable archives contain font files or screenshots of user skins.

0.3.2 also includes unmodified Mojang boat-oak, shield and nether-brick reference PNGs
under `upstream/equipment_textures/` in the source bundle for reproducible previews.
They are subject to the same Mojang copyright and Minecraft EULA as other attributed
Mojang sample assets. Runtime side-item/armor/tool textures are referenced from the
game rather than replaced by new vanilla texture atlases.

## 0.4.3 artwork and poses

The green option-3 Morpher artwork was generated and selected in this conversation;
